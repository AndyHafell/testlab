# Flight Sim (3D jet + air combat)

The exact one-shot prompt, fired at both `openrouter/fusion` and `anthropic/claude-opus-4.8` — same prompt, no follow-ups, no retries:

```
Build a 3D flight sim in the browser — one runnable HTML file where I'm ripping through the sky in a fighter jet. Nail the flight feel first: throttle up and down, pitch, roll and yaw, bank into turns, climb and dive, with enough weight and momentum that it feels like flying a real jet and not a paper plane. An open sky over some terrain — ground, water, maybe a few mountains or a city below — with a real sense of speed and altitude.

Then make it a combat flight sim: enemy jets in the air to chase down and take out, with a gun and lockable missiles, a targeting reticle and lock-on box, and flares to dodge incoming missiles. Let me get into proper turning fights — get on someone's tail, lock, fire, watch them trail smoke and go down. Throw in a few ground targets to strafe if it's easy, and enemies that actually maneuver instead of flying straight.

Give me the cockpit feel: a clean HUD with airspeed, altitude, a heading/compass, throttle, target info and a radar/minimap, plus a switch between cockpit view and a chase cam behind the jet. Make it juicy — afterburner whoosh, missile-lock beeping, explosions, sound synthesized in code. Libraries from a CDN like three.js are totally fine; just no bundled asset files. One runnable HTML file.
```
