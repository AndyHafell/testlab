# Destruction Sandbox (physics mayhem playground)

The exact one-shot prompt, fired at both `openrouter/fusion` and `anthropic/claude-opus-4.8` — same prompt, no follow-ups, no retries:

```
Build a physics destruction sandbox in the browser — one runnable HTML file, no goal, just a playground for mayhem. Give me a world with real, satisfying physics where I can spawn stuff — crates, balls, planks, barrels, ragdoll dudes, towers of blocks — and then wreck it all. Everything should stack, topple, collide and tumble convincingly, with weight and momentum, so knocking down a tower or watching a pile collapse just feels good.

Hand me a toolbox to cause chaos: drag and fling objects around with the mouse, drop bombs and barrels that explode with proper blast force and send everything flying, a gravity / anti-gravity tool, maybe a wind or black-hole tool that sucks everything in. Explosions should chain — light one barrel near others and the whole thing goes up. Let me spawn presets too, like a brick wall, a Jenga tower, or a crowd of ragdolls, so there's always something fun to demolish.

Make it feel amazing to play with: screen shake and a slow-mo toggle so I can watch the carnage in detail, debris and particles, satisfying impact and explosion sounds synthesized in code, and a quick reset button. A little UI to pick the current tool and spawn objects, but otherwise just let me make a mess. A physics library from a CDN (like Matter.js) is fine; no bundled asset files. One runnable HTML file.
```
