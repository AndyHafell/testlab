# GTA v2 (open-world + weapons)

The exact one-shot prompt, fired at both `openrouter/fusion` and `anthropic/claude-opus-4.8` — same prompt, no follow-ups, no retries:

```
Build a GTA-style open-world game in one runnable HTML file — top-down, set in a small city you can actually mess around in. You're on foot by default: walk with WASD, and jack any parked or moving car to drive it, with smooth physics — real acceleration, weight, and drift when you yank the wheel. The streets are alive: pedestrians stroll the sidewalks, traffic flows and stops at lights, and everyone reacts when things kick off — they scatter, cars swerve, chaos spreads. Get out whenever you want and go back on foot.

This time it's all about the weapons. Give me a proper arsenal I can cycle through — fists, a pistol, an SMG, a shotgun, and something heavy like a rocket launcher — switched with number keys or a weapon wheel, each with its own feel, fire rate, range, and ammo count. Aim with the mouse and shoot with click; let me fire on foot and pull off drive-bys from the car. Bullets should actually land — on pedestrians, gang members, cops, tires, gas tanks — with blood, sparks, shell casings, muzzle flash, and cars that catch fire and blow up if you light them up enough. Scatter weapon, ammo, health and armor pickups around the map so there's a reason to explore, and drop in a couple of enemy gangs that'll shoot back if you start something on their turf.

Tie it all into a wanted system that escalates with how much hell you raise: pop a couple stars and cops chase and shoot, push it higher and you get roadblocks and SWAT, then break line of sight and lay low to cool off and lose them. Give me a clean HUD — health, armor, current weapon and ammo, wanted stars — plus a live minimap showing streets, cops, and pickups. Make it feel juicy to just rampage around in: screen shake on explosions, satisfying sound effects synthesized in code, the works. One file, runs straight in the browser.
```
