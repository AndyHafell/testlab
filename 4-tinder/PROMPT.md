# Tinder for video ideas (demo · real data)

The exact one-shot prompt, as given to Claude Fable 5 — no follow-ups, no retries:

```
Build a Tinder for video ideas — one runnable app. I'll feed you a JSON of my real ideas backlog — each idea has a title, hook, format, and a 16-bit pixel-art thumbnail in my channel's style. One idea at a time as a big swipeable card, thumbnail as the hero — swipe right to add to the content calendar, left to archive, arrow keys too, satisfying swipe physics. When the deck runs out, it builds my filming schedule for the week as a calendar I can drag to reorder.
```
