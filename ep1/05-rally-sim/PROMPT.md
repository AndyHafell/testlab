# Rally driving sim (demo)

The exact one-shot prompt, as given to Claude Fable 5 — no follow-ups, no retries:

```
Build a rally driving game that plays great on iPhone — one runnable file, mobile-first. Touch controls (on-screen pedals and steering, or tilt to steer). A third-person chase cam behind a rally car ripping down a dirt stage — drift on the loose surface, kick up dust, scenery flying by. Checkpoints and a stage timer, a speedo HUD, and a finish screen with your time. Runs smooth in mobile Safari, in portrait or landscape.
```
