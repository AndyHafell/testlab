# Feedback Lab (build-on-established · real data)

The exact one-shot prompt, as given to Claude Fable 5 — no follow-ups, no retries:

```
Last video you built my intelligence dashboard. Now close the loop: add a "Feedback" tab to my creator dashboard, far right in the nav. Inside, a Feedback Lab: an A/B test tracker — each video's title and thumbnail variants side by side with watch-time share bars and the winner crowned (I'll feed you my real test data from the last video). A daily check-in: every morning after a publish I drop the 24-hour Studio screenshot, and it extracts views vs typical band, CTR, AVD, and the 1-of-10 ranking onto that video's card. Then the payoff: everything the Feedback Lab learns feeds the AI reviewer on my Publish page — so before I publish the next video, it scores my packaging against what's actually been winning and predicts the ranking.
```
